## Intent

- Objective: Upgrade the complete BoundedFreedom Skill package to an Astra-aware hierarchical router that increases use of the balanced Terra lane for eligible work, reduces standing context, steps down after difficult phases, and measures cost per accepted work unit.
- Non-goals: Do not make Astra or Terra a task-wide default, raise account-level model limits, replace the four execution contracts, weaken S0-S4 assurance, alter scientific methods, add a standing agent team, or claim unmeasured savings.

Official model guidance supports explicit delegation bounds, compact context, variable reasoning effort, and proportional verification; local break-even points still require a forward calibration window.
